# Code of conduct

Hermes HQ is built in the open, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews)
is expected to keep it a good place to work.

**Do**

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent. Many people here are new to open source or to Swift.
- Criticise code and ideas, never the person.
- Keep to the point of the issue or pull request.
- Respect people's privacy: don't post anyone's personal details, chats or screenshots without their permission.

**Don't**

- Harass, insult or demean anyone, for any reason, including identity, background, experience or opinion.
- Make sexual comments, threats or personal attacks.
- Keep pushing after someone has asked you to stop.
- Spam, or advertise unrelated products.

**If something goes wrong**

Report it privately through the repository's **Security** tab (Report a vulnerability works for conduct reports too),
or ask the maintainer to get in touch on the issue without giving details. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.
Serious or repeated cases lead to a permanent ban from the project.
