# Name and icon policy

The source code of Hermes HQ is free software under the GNU General Public License v3.0. The name **"Hermes HQ"**,
the app's icons and its logo are not covered by that license. They identify the official app made by the project's
maintainer.

**You may:**

- build and run the app from this source for yourself, under the name it has;
- say that your project is "based on Hermes HQ" or "a fork of Hermes HQ";
- use the name to refer to the official app, for example in a review, an article or a tutorial.

**Please don't, without written permission:**

- publish a build (on the App Store, TestFlight, a website or anywhere else) under the name "Hermes HQ" or a confusingly
  similar name, or with the app's icons;
- suggest that your project, product or service is official, or endorsed by the maintainer.

If you publish a fork, give it its own name and icon, and change the bundle identifiers in `project.yml`.

"Hermes" and "Hermes Agent" are associated with Nous Research. Hermes HQ is an independent project and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Nous Research.

Questions? Open an issue.
